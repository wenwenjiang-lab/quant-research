# Statistical Analysis of Nasdaq-100 Futures (MNQ)

## Overview

This repository documents my independent quantitative research into intraday behavior of Nasdaq-100 Futures (MNQ).

The objective is to investigate whether statistically significant market behaviors exist through hypothesis-driven research rather than discretionary trading.

This repository serves as a long-term research portfolio covering systematic trading, market microstructure, and statistical analysis.

---

# Research Topics

Current research focuses on:

- Opening Range
- VWAP Interaction
- Momentum Continuation
- Volatility Expansion
- Session Statistics
- Market Microstructure

Future research:

- Alpha Research
- Feature Engineering
- Machine Learning
- Portfolio Construction
- Market Regime Detection

---

# Research Workflow

Every project follows the scientific method.

1. Define a research hypothesis
2. Collect historical market data
3. Clean and validate data
4. Perform statistical analysis
5. Evaluate significance
6. Document findings
7. Draw conclusions
