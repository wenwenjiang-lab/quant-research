\## What drives the model?



The frozen forecasting process uses only information intended to be available before the forecast timestamp. Its model context includes:



\- chronological Elo-derived team strength;

\- a fixed home-field adjustment;

\- rolling run-differential information;

\- rolling win-rate differences;

\- rest-day differences;

\- seven-day schedule load;

\- available pregame pitcher-confirmation information.



The published probability reflects the combined model output. Individual matchup explanations are not created after outcomes are known.



\## Research integrity



Every forecast is:



\- generated before the eligible game begins;

\- timestamped;

\- linked to a frozen model version;

\- stored in an append-only ledger;

\- retained whether correct or incorrect;

\- evaluated using probability-sensitive metrics, including log loss and Brier score.



The purpose is not to advertise betting selections. The purpose is to conduct a prospective forecasting experiment in which the evidence is committed before the outcomes are observed.



\## Interpretation boundary



One successful day would not validate the model, and one unsuccessful day would not invalidate it.



The study will be evaluated over a larger prospective sample using:



\- log loss;

\- Brier score;

\- calibration;

\- comparison with transparent baselines;

\- protocol-deviation reporting.



This project does not claim profitable betting performance or guaranteed prediction accuracy.
